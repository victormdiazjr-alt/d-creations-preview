# Official Chat Map

## 00 — D Creations Control Center

Owns priorities, approvals, architecture decisions, and final coordination. All specialist chats return concise summaries here.

## 10 — Storefront Builder

Owns implementation, responsive behavior, editable source reconstruction, and Shopify theme integration.

## 20 — Content & SEO Studio

Owns navigation language, product descriptions, collections, metadata, FAQs, calls to action, and Puerto Rico–appropriate brand voice.

## 30 — Visual & Image Studio

Owns art direction, image generation briefs, asset selection, product imagery, brand consistency, and responsive image variants.

## 40 — QA, Conversion & Accessibility Audit

Owns read-only audits for broken flows, visual regressions, accessibility, SEO implementation, trust, and conversion friction.

## Coordination rules

- The control center decides priorities.
- Only Storefront Builder edits implementation files unless ownership is explicitly reassigned.
- Content and Visual Studios prepare approved inputs; they do not silently change production code.
- QA audits milestones rather than every minor edit.
- Efficiency review happens before broad parallel work, not after tokens have already been spent.
